# Vehicle Rental System

---

## 1. How to Rent a Vehicle

1. Go to a **vehicle rental location** (marked on the map).
2. Approach the vehicle rental marker/pickup.
3. Press **`Y`** to open the **Rental Menu**.
4. Choose a vehicle type from the available list.
5. Select rental duration (up to **24 hours**).
6. Confirm and pay the rental fee.

> ⚠ You must have enough money in hand or bank to rent a vehicle.

---

## 2. Rental Rules & Duration

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Maximum rental time | 24 hours |
| After time expires | Vehicle **automatically despawns**, regardless of location |
| Rented vehicles visible in | **`HOME` Menu → Vehicles tab** |
| Engine & lock usage | Same as owned vehicles (engine = Y hold, lock = L) |
