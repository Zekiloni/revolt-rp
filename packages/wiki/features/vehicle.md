# Vehicle System

---

## 1. Buying a Vehicle

- Vehicles are purchased at **dealership locations**.
- After purchase, your vehicle appears in:
  - **`HOME` → Vehicles menu**
  - You can spawn/despawn it from there.
- You must visit **DMV** to register the vehicle legally.

---

## 2. Vehicle Registration (DMV)

After buying a vehicle:
1. Drive to the **DMV**.
2. Press **`Y`** to open the vehicle registration menu.
3. Pay the registration fee.

---

## 3. Locking & Doors

| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Lock/Unlock vehicle | **L** |
| Open trunk/hood | Stand near → **Y (Tap)** |
| Open/close windows | **/windows [index]** |
| Open vehicle interaction menu | **Y (Tap)** near vehicle |

---

## 4. Engine & Interior Controls

| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Start/Stop engine | **Y (Hold 2 seconds)** |
| Seatbelt | **B** |
| Turn signals | **Left / Right Arrow Keys** |
| Hazard lights  | **Q** |
| Vehicle settings (while inside) | Quick **Y menu** |

---

## 5. Vehicle Inventory (Trunk / Hood Storage)

- Open by standing near trunk or hood and pressing **`Y`**.
- Opens a **vehicle inventory UI**.
- You can:
  - Move items from player inventory to trunk.
  - Store weapons, bags, boxes, etc.
  - Retrieve items from trunk to inventory.

> ⚠ Some items cannot be stored (e.g., large objects).

---

## 6. Player-to-Player Vehicle Sell/Trade

To sell your vehicle:
1. Be inside your vehicle with the buyer nearby.
2. Press **Y** → Vehicle Options → Sell / Transfer.
3. Buyer must **accept the transfer**.
4. Vehicle ownership moves to buyer’s account.
