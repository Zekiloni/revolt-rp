# DMV & Driver’s License System

The **DMV (Department of Motor Vehicles)** is responsible for issuing driver’s licenses to players through a realistic theory and practical exam process and for vehicle registrations.

---

## 1. How to Start

1. Go to the **DMV Office** (marked on the map).
2. Approach the **DMV desk/checkpoint**.
3. Press **`Y`** to open the DMV menu.
4. Select **“Obtain Driver’s License”**.

---

## 2. Theory Test (Written Exam)

- You will be given a set of **multiple-choice questions**.
- Questions are based on:
  - Road signs
  - Traffic rules
  - Right-of-way laws
  - Speed limits
  - Basic vehicle safety
- If you answer enough questions correctly → you pass.
- If you fail → you must pay the fee again to retry.

> 💡 *Questions are formed based on common knowledge.*

---

## 3. Practical Driving Test

After passing the theory test, you proceed to the **driving exam** with a DMV instructor NPC.

### During the driving test:
- You must follow the **GPS route/checkpoints**.
- The system monitors:
  | Rule | What is checked |
  |------|------------------|
  | **Speed** | Driving too fast or too slow |
  | **Damage** | Crashing or hitting objects/pedestrians |
  | **Lane & Stops** | Ignoring stop signs, red lights, lane discipline |
- Too many mistakes = **You fail**.

---

## 4. After Passing the Exam

If successful:
- You receive your **Driver’s License** (item stored in inventory).
- License is automatically linked to your character profile.
