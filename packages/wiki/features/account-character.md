# Account & Character System

Welcome to **Revolt Roleplay (RRP)**.  
This page explains how to access the server, create characters, and follow the core standards for realistic roleplay identity.

---

## 1. Logging In & Account Application

RRP uses a **Master Account System** with option **Discord authentication**.

- Players log in through the **User Control Panel (UCP)** via email or discord.
- One Master Account = up to **3 in-game characters**.
- Each character has its own inventory, money, job, phone number, vehicles, and properties.

- Every master account must be approved by UCP Testers. Your roleplay knowledge, standard and general server rules will be put to test.

> ⚠ **Important:** Using multiple Discord accounts to bypass the 3-character limit is strictly forbidden and detectable.

---

## 2. Character Creation

After logging in, players can create a new character through the **Character Creator UI**.

### Appearance (Character Creator UI)

You will be able to customize:
- **Face structure & heritage**  
- **Skin tone, hair, facial hair**
- **Eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jaw, chin**
- **Body type & basic clothing**

---

## 3. Character Background & Origin Rules

Every character on Revolt Roleplay must respect a **realistic background** that fits GTA V / San Andreas / U.S. lore.

### Allowed:
- Born in **any realistic U.S. state** or small lore towns (Harmony, Sandy Shores, Grapeseed, etc.).
- Immigrants are allowed but must include:
  - Country of origin  
  - Immigration reason & legal status  
  - Basic timeline (when did they arrive)

### Not allowed:
- Superpowers, military generals at age 18, millionaire gangsters, secret agents, princes of Dubai, etc.
- Copying real-life celebrities, criminals, or movie characters.
- “GTA Online style” unrealistic names like *Xx_KillerBoy_xX*.

## 4. Background story
- At character creation stage you will be asked to input a short story that will be displayed within UCP and reviewed by UCP Testers.
