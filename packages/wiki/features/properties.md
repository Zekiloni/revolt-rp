# Property & Business System

---

## 1 Property Categories

### • Residential
Private housing for characters.
- **Examples:** houses, trailers, apartments, motel rooms.

### • Commercial
Player- or organization-run businesses open to customers.
- **Examples:** shops, bars, clubs, services.

### • Garage
Standalone garages or storage bays for vehicles.

### • Public Service
Government & emergency service facilities.
- **Examples:** DMV, City Hall, hospital, police/fire stations, banks, post office.

### • Industrial
Heavy-use or production-focused areas.
- **Examples:** warehouses, depots, plants, yards.

### • Utility
Support infrastructure for the world.
- **Examples:** recycling center, utilities service points.

### • Other
Everything that doesn’t fit the above (special interiors, event spaces).

## 2 Commercial Subtypes (Business Kinds)

> This is the **expanded list** of commercial property types you can own and operate.

- **Grocery Store** – snacks, drinks, everyday items.
- **Gas Station** – fuel, convenience goods; potential vehicle services.
- **Ammunition** – (legal) guns & ammo; requires licensing & compliance.
- **Clothing Store** – outfits, accessories, masks.
- **Furniture Store** – furniture & decor (future interior placement).
- **Jewelry Store** – watches, rings, valuables (high risk, high reward).
- **Electronics Store** – phones, radios, gadgets.
- **Mechanic Garage** – repairs, upgrades, inspections.
- **Pharmacy** – medicine, bandages, medical supplies (legal compliance).
- **Vehicle Dealership** – sell vehicles to players.
- **Tattoo Shop** – tattoos & body art services.
- **Barber Shop** – haircuts, grooming.
- **Cafe/Bar** – beverages, snacks, RP hangouts.
- **Night Club** – music, events, paid entry, VIP.
- **Vehicle Rent** – rental vehicles (hour-based).
- **Restaurant** – cooked meals, menus, seating.
- **Fast Food** – quick meals, takeout windows.
- **Casino** – gambling (high regulation, staff oversight).

## 5 Buying Properties

- Find a **For Sale** sign / real-estate marker and press **`Y`**.
- Confirm purchase → property appears in **Home → Properties** panel.
- Properties may be owned by **players or organizations**.

---

## 6 Managing Properties

**Core interactions**
- **Lock/Unlock:** stand by the door → press **`L`**.
- **Access control:** grant entry to friends, faction or organization members.
- **Storage:** keep items in the property’s interior stash.
- **Interior & furniture:** feature for placement and design.

**Business management (Commercial)**
- Set **product prices**.
- Add/remove **stock** (owner or authorized staff).
- Manage **employees & ranks** via the organization panel.
- Earn revenue when players purchase items from your catalog.

---

## 7 Shop Inventory & Items

Businesses sell items from their property inventory.

- Customers browse a **catalog/menu** and add items to the cart.
- Items can be **stackable** (ammo, materials) or **non-stackable** (beer bottle, unique items).
  - *Example:* **Beer** is non-stackable — buying 2 beers gives **two separate items**.
- Owners restock by **moving items into business storage** (via management UI).
