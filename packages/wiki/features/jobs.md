# Jobs

---
## 1. Garbage collector
### How to Start the Job

1. Go to the **Recycling Center** (job location).
2. Approach the **job marker** and press **`Y`**.
3. Select **“Start Job”**.
4. You will receive:
   - A **job outfit** if you equip it at the job equipment location 
   - A **garbage truck**
   - A list of **trash container checkpoints** on the map

---

### Collecting Trash

- Drive the garbage truck to the **marked trash containers**.
- Exit the vehicle and press **`Y`** near a trash bin to pick up garbage bags.
- Carry the garbage to the **back of the truck** and press **Left Click (LMB)** to load it.
- Each garbage bag adds weight to the truck’s load.

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Minimum load to deliver | **12 kg** of trash |
| Maximum load | **~115 kg** |
| Truck full | Return to Recycling Center →

---

### Delivering & Getting Paid

1. Once the truck is loaded (12–115 kg), go back to the **Recycling Center Delivery Point**.
2. Park in the marked area.
3. Your character will **freeze in an unloading animation**.
4. After a few seconds — you receive **payment based on total weight** of collected trash.
