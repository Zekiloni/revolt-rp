# Weapons & Ammunition System

---

## 1. Equipping Weapons

- Weapons must be stored in the **inventory**.
- To equip:
  - Place the weapon in a **quick slot (1–5)**.
  - Press the matching key to draw the weapon.
- Press the same key again to **holster** the weapon.
- Weapons cannot be fired if:
  - No ammunition is loaded.
  - Weapon is broken or jammed.
  - You are handcuffed or injured.

---

## 2. Ammunition System

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Ammo types | 9mm, .45 ACP, 5.56mm, 7.62mm, shotgun shells, etc. |
| Ammo storage | Stored as items in inventory (stackable) |
| Reloading | Press **R**, requires correct ammo type in inventory and weapon in hand |

---

## 3. Reloading Weapons

**Reload Key: `R`**

Requirements:
- Weapon must be equipped in hand.
- Correct ammo type must exist in inventory.
- If valid, ammo is reduced and weapon is reloaded.

Example:
Pistol (0/12) + 9mm Ammo (x24) → Press R → Pistol (12/12), Ammo (x12)

---

## 4. Weapon Damage & Injuries (Future Development)

- Weapon damage will depend on:
  - Caliber
  - Distance
  - Body part hit
- Wearing armor (vests) reduces damage.
- Headshots & chest shots are more lethal.
