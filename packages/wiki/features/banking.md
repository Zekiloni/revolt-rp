# Banking System

---

## 1. Bank Cards & Accounts

- Every character receives a **personal bank account and a unique bank card** upon character creation.
- Cards can be:
  - Deactivated
  - Replaced (new card issued)
  - Given to other players (card is a physical **identity item**)
- Each card has a **unique ID and PIN code**.

> ⚠ Card sharing should be roleplayed realistically and can be traced by law enforcement.

---

## 2. Accessing the Bank

- Go to a **Bank Interior or ATM checkpoint**
- Press **`Y`** to open the **Bank Menu** at bank or **hold your card** and press **`Y`** at **ATM**.
- You will see:
  - **Account Overview (Balance, Account Number, Card Status)**
  - **Transactions History**
  - **Account Settings**

---

## 3. Account Settings

Inside the bank settings menu, you can:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| View PIN Code | Shows your current 4-digit PIN |
| Change PIN | Set a new PIN for your card |
| Deactivate Card | Temporarily disable access |
| Add New Card | Issue a new personal or secondary card |
| Link Phone Number | Enables mobile banking features |
| Unlink Device | Remove phone access |

---

## 4. Mobile Banking & Phone Transfers

Once your **phone number is linked** to the bank account:
- You can use the **Wallet / Banking app** on your in-game phone.
- Features include:
  - **Check account balance**
  - **Transfer money to players or businesses**
  - **View latest transactions**
  - **Pay fines or bills**

> 📌 If the phone is stolen and not unlinked, someone can access your money.

---

## 5. Savings Accounts

Players can open an optional **Savings Account**.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Interest | Generates passive income over time (server-set rate) |
| Lock Duration | Money cannot be withdrawn before the end of contract |
| Purpose | Long-term financial planning, legal businesses, factions |

---

## 7. Quick Summary Table

| Feature | Key / Location |
|---------|------------------|
| Open Bank Menu | `Y` at a bank or ATM (while holding Bank Card) |
| Check Balance | Bank → Account Overview |
| Change PIN | Bank → Settings |
| New Card | Bank → Settings → Add Card |
| Link Phone | Bank → Settings → Link Phone |
| Phone Transfers | Phone → Banking App |
