## How to Connect to the Server

### 1. Download RAGE Multiplayer
- Go to **https://rage.mp/**
- Download and install the latest **Client** version.
- Make sure GTA V is installed (Steam, Epic Games or Rockstar launcher).

### 2. Launch RAGE:MP
- Open `RAGE Multiplayer`
- Let it update (if required)

### 3. Connect to RRP
####  Option A — Direct IP:
1. Click **Direct Connect** (bottom right in launcher)  
2. Enter IP
3. Press **Connect**

#### Option B — Favorites:
1. Open **Favorites** tab in RAGE:MP  
2. Click **Add Server**  
3. Paste the server IP → `127.0.0.1:22520`  
4. Server will appear in your list → double-click to join

## Troubleshooting / Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| *Server doesn't show up* | Use **Direct Connect** with IP manually |
| *Infinite loading screen* | Restart RAGE:MP and verify GTA V game files |
| *Game crashes on startup* | Delete `RAGEMP/client_packages` and restart |

## Server Lore

Once known as a dusty, forgotten corner of Sandy Shores, the town has undergone major redevelopment over the past decade. With new investment, modern housing, a renovated pier and lakeside promenade, Revolt has transformed into a small but growing coastal city.

Yet beneath the polished concrete, neon lights and beachfront cafes – the old town still breathes. Trailer parks, small-time crime, generational families, veterans, bikers and political tensions create a fragile balance between progress and tradition.

Key Lore Points:
• Location: Northern coast of the Alamo Sea, Blaine County
• Population: ~18,000 residents
• Government: City Council & Mayor, under San Andreas State jurisdiction
• Economy: Fishing, tourism, small businesses, construction, minor industry
• Crime: Street gangs, organized smuggling across the lake, drug trafficking, local biker clubs
• Law Enforcement: Blaine County Sheriff’s Office – Revolt Station
• Emergency Services: Revolt Medical Center & Fire Rescue Department

This is not Los Santos. This is not luxury or fame.
This is Revolt – where every face is familiar, every mistake remembered.
