# 2 Core Principles & Global Rules

These rules apply to every player at all times — in-game, on Discord, and on any platform connected to Revolt Roleplay.

---

## 2.1 Common Courtesy & Respect

- Treat all players with respect, both IC and OOC.
- Racism, hate speech, harassment or bullying is **strictly prohibited**.
- Real-life threats or doxxing = **instant permanent ban**.
- Do not harass other players in DMs, Discord or any external platform in relation to in-game events.
- Arguments should be handled maturely. If you cannot solve it peacefully, make a report.

---

## 2.2 In-Character (IC) vs Out-of-Character (OOC)

- **IC is IC, OOC is OOC.**  
  Do not mix real-life emotions into in-character interactions.

- **/b or /ooc chat is only for quick clarification.**  
  Not for arguments, drama or long conversations.

- **No OOC insults in /b, /pm, or Discord.**

- **Do not reveal OOC information IC.** Example: saying “he is lagging”, “admin is watching”, “your ping is bad” in-character.

---

## 2.3 Discord, External Communication & Screenshots

- Using Discord, PMs, streams, or voice calls to gain IC advantages is **metagaming**.
- You may discuss RP ideas or planning OOC, but final decisions must happen IC.
- Taking screenshots or recordings is allowed, but:
  - Do **not post unblurred player names, IDs or private conversations without consent**.
  - Do **not use screens from someone else's POV as IC information** unless realistically obtained IC.

---

## 2.4 Metagaming

**Metagaming = using OOC information in character. This is a serious rule break.**

Examples of metagaming:
- Using Discord call to tell someone where you died, where enemies are hiding, police movements, etc.
- Checking a stream to find another player's location.
- Calling someone IC about something you only know from OOC chat.
- Using the player’s nametag or ID to identify them without seeing their face IC.

---

## 2.5 Powergaming

**Powergaming = performing unrealistic or forced actions without giving others a chance to react.**

Examples:
- `/me punches him, knocks him out, takes his gun and runs away` — without letting the other player respond.
- Forcing injuries on someone without RP (e.g. “/me breaks your arm”).
- RP’ing superhuman strength (flipping cars, surviving headshots, running after being shot multiple times).
- Refusing to roleplay injuries after being shot, crashed or severely harmed.
- Roleplaying knowledge your character could not realistically have.

---

## 2.6 Fear Roleplay (FearRP)

- Your character must value their life and act as a real person would in dangerous situations.
- If a gun is aimed at your head at close range, you **cannot** casually pull your weapon or run away.
- You must **put your hands up or follow instructions** when realistically afraid.
- Jumping off cliffs, provoking armed police, standing in front of speeding cars = NonRP.

**FearRP Example:**
Good RP: `/me slowly raises his hands, visibly shaking as the gun is aimed at him.`  
Bad RP: `/me takes out a pistol and shoots while 3 deputies aim AR-15s at him.`

---

## 2.7 Character Realism

- Your character must have a believable background and act accordingly.
- 17-year-olds cannot be cartel bosses or own military-grade weapons.
- If your character is injured, depressed, addicted or traumatized — roleplay consequences realistically.
- No supernatural powers, superheroes or military-grade training without approved background.

---

## 2.8 Admin Interaction

- Use non urgent channels of communictation for questions or non-urgent issues.
- Use instant channels for rule-breaking or urgent admin assistance.
- Lying to staff, refusing to RP, or ignoring admin instructions will result in punishment.
- Admin decisions must be followed immediately — you may appeal later on Discord or forums.
