# 10 Sexual & Disgusting Roleplay Rules (ERP & Extreme RP)

Sexual or explicit roleplay is allowed on Revolt Roleplay only under strict conditions. ERP (Erotic Roleplay) and any disturbing or explicit content must remain mature, consensual, private and respectful of other players.

---

## 10.1 Consent (IC & OOC Required)

- Both players (or all players that are able to see the interraction) must agree to sexual or intimate roleplay.
- If a player says **“Stop”**, **“No”**, or expresses discomfort OOC → you must stop immediately.
- Consent must be respected at all times — **no forcing, no manipulation, no pressure.**
- It is forbidden to include IC or OOC minors in any type of sexual roleplay.

---

## 10.2 Forbidden Content (Instant Ban)

The following is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate admin action:

**Sexual Content Involving:**
- Minors (under 18 IC or OOC)
- Non-consensual acts (rape, coercion or forced prostitution)
- Incest or family-related sexual acts
- Bestiality or animals
- Necrophilia
- Sexual slavery or sex trafficking RP of minors
- Erotic roleplay in public places or near unwilling players

---

## 10.3 Disgusting / Abnormal

Forbidden.

**This includes:**
- Vore, cannibalism involving sexual context
- Scat, urine fetish, vomit fetish
- Sexual mutilation, genital harm, extreme torture erotica
- Gore-focused sexual behavior
- Use of body fluids (blood, feces, urine, vomit) as fetish
- Slavery, forced breeding, humiliating sexual control

**If any of the above is attempted → immediate punishment.**  
Admins may void or punish scenes that are too graphic, disturbing, or abusive.

---

## 10.4 Allowed but Must Be Faded-to-Black

Even with consent:
- Detailed sexual emotes should be avoided in public.
- Fade-to-black (F2B) or discreet language is recommended.
- Erotic RP must happen in **private spaces**: interior, house, motel room, private chat or away from others.  

---

## 10.5 Pregnancy & Family-Related RP

Allowed only if:
- Both players OOC consent.
- Character is **18+ IC**.
- No pregnancy fetish RP involving minors.
- Miscarriages, abortion RP or child illness RP requires all sides to agree OOC and admin approval.

---

## 10.6 Admin Intervention

Admins may stop or punish sexual RP if:
- It violates the rules above.
- It is conducted in public or non-private places.
- It becomes overly graphic or disturbing.
- It involves manipulation, OOC blackmail or harassment.

Punishments may include:  
**IC void → admin jail → permanent ban.**
