# 9 Rules of Engagement (Faction Conflict System)

The Rules of Engagement (RoE) define how conflicts between factions are allowed to escalate on Revolt Roleplay. They exist to ensure that faction wars are realistic, fair, story-driven and do not devolve into deathmatching or rule abuse.

These rules apply to all legal and illegal factions and are enforced by the **Faction Management (FM)**.

---

## 9.1 What Counts as a Faction Conflict?

A conflict begins when one faction takes a hostile action against another.  
This can include:

- Tagging or vandalizing enemy property or turf  
- Assaulting or kidnapping faction members  
- Extortion attempts or threats of violence  
- Shootings, ambushes, or lethal force

Not all conflicts are immediately wars. They escalate over time.  
The highest stage is **Faction War**, which must occur through **organic IC escalation** — not instantly.

---

## 9.2 Participation Requirements

To prevent throwaway characters, alt abuse or instant wars, the following rules apply:

| Participation Rule | Requirement |
|--------------------|-------------|
| Minimum faction age | The faction must exist for at least **30 days** before initiating conflict and must be official. |
| Characters allowed in conflict | Must be in the faction for **7+ days** and part of the roster. |
| New factions under 30 days | Cannot start conflict, but **may defend themselves** if attacked first. |
| Character switching | You must use your **main character** in that faction. Alts are not permitted in war. |

Factions found creating fresh characters just for wars risk **immediate faction shutdown by Faction Management**.

---

## 9.3 Faction Conflict Player Kills (Conflict-PK)

A **Conflict PK** occurs when a faction member is killed due to the ongoing conflict or by law enforcement while committing or fleeing from an attack.

After being conflict-PK’d:
- The player cannot rejoin the conflict, even using another character.
- They cannot enter enemy turf or scout on behalf of their faction.
- They may acknowledge the faction is at war, but **cannot participate, strategize or share info**.

They **may only defend** their faction if an attack happens in front of them and their life is in danger.

Rejoining after a PK = punishment for the player & possible action against the faction by FMT.

---

## 9.4 Escalation to Full Faction War

A conflict becomes a **Faction War** when:
- Multiple killings occur  
- Both factions engage in lethal force  
- Tension can no longer be resolved peacefully  

Once a war officially starts:
- Both faction leaders must notify **Faction Management** and seek approval.
- It is encouraged that leaders begin talks for war-ending conditions (hostage trades, money, ceasefire terms).

---

## 9.5 Attacks During War

An **attack** is **any offensive action where the enemy is likely to be injured or killed**.

### A valid attack:
- Drive-by shooting with masks
- Kidnapping a rival gang member
- Planned home invasion / ambush
- Armed robbery with intent to harm

### Not an attack:
- Verbal arguments, fist fights, minor provocations — unless escalated to serious levels.

### Attack Rules:
| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Cooldown | **6 hours** between attacks initiated by the same faction |
| Max participants per attack | **under 7 players** (more requires strong IC reason and FM approval) |
| Vehicles | Must be realistic (no supercars, no off-road bikes for city attacks without reason) |
| Blockwiping | **Strictly forbidden** — no mass executions, no traps blocking all exits |
| Looting dead bodies | **Not allowed** during wars |
| Scouting | Must be done realistically — not hovering in enemy turf for hours |

**Using more than 67 attackers or 2 vehicles requires valid RP justification and will be reviewed if reported.**

---

## 9.6 Defense Rules

A defense is when a faction is actively being attacked.

Defenders may:
- Use firearms to protect themselves once attackers are identified  
- Retreat or surrender

Defenders may NOT:
- Sit on rooftops waiting for hours  
- Place vehicles as deliberate barricades to funnel attackers  
- Patrol the entire turf constantly with visible weapons

---

## 9.7 Allies & Third-Party Support

- Factions may only ally in war if they have **IC reasoning and history**.
- Alliances must be reported to **Faction Management** before joining combat.
- Allied factions **share PK lists and cooldown timers**.

### Mercenaries:
- Hiring paid shooters for wars is forbidden.
- Hiring assassins for **individual hits during peace time** is allowed — but not during active faction wars.

---

## 9.8 End of War

A war ends when:
- Both leaders agree on terms  
- One faction surrenders or is wiped out IC  
- Faction Management enforces a ceasefire 

Terms must be respected — breaking an agreed ceasefire is a serious offense.

---

**These Rules of Engagement exist to protect faction roleplay from chaos, deathmatching and OOC conflict. Realistic escalation, story-building and consequences are always the priority.**
