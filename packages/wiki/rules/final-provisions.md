# 13 Final Provisions & Rule Acknowledgement

This final section explains how rules are enforced, how players accept them by playing on Revolt Roleplay, and how staff decisions are handled.

---

## 13.1 Rule Acceptance

By joining Revolt Roleplay, you automatically agree to:
- Follow all rules listed in this document.
- Accept that ignorance of the rules is **not an excuse**.
- Understand that any character, property or asset can be removed if rules are broken.
- Respect server staff and their decisions.

Using the server equals a **binding agreement** to this rulebook.

---

## 13.2 Staff Authority & Final Say

- Staff members (Support, Admins, Faction Management, Developers) represent the server’s authority.
- Their decisions **must be followed immediately**, even if you disagree.
- Arguing during the situation is not allowed — you may appeal after.
- Public disrespect, staff harassment or “admin baiting” is punishable.

---

## 13.3 Appeals & Reports

You may appeal:
- Bans, character kills, admin actions — via forum.
- CK or PK decisions — if you believe they were invalid or abusive.
- Faction strikes or removals — through proper channels.

When appealing:
- Stay respectful  
- Provide evidence (logs, screenshots)  
- Do not falsify proof — this will result in harsher punishment.

---

## 13.4 Rule Changes & Updates

- Rules can be updated, removed or rewritten by server management at any time.
- Major changes will be announced on Discord and in announcements.
- Players are responsible for staying informed.

---

## 13.5 Liability & Character Ownership

- All in-game content (characters, money, assets) is a virtual part of the server.
- Staff may confiscate or delete these if earned illegally or through rule-breaking.
- **You do not “own” your character absolutely** — it exists within server rules and story.

---

## 13.6 Final Statement

Revolt Roleplay is built on:
- **Realism**
- **Story-driven roleplay**
- **Respect for other players**
- **Consequences for actions**

If you follow this rulebook, you help create a world that feels alive, believable and enjoyable for everyone.

**Thank you for being part of Revolt Roleplay.**
