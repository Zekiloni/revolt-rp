# 4 Crime, Violence & Conflict Rules

This section defines how criminal actions, violence and conflicts must be roleplayed. Revolt Roleplay enforces realistic crime — no GTA:O behavior, no random shootings, no unrealistic robberies.

---

## 4.1 Deathmatching & Random Killing

**Deathmatching (DM)** = attacking or killing another player without valid roleplay reason or escalation.

You are breaking this rule if:
- You shoot or fight someone without any prior IC interaction.
- You kill because someone insulted you once.
- You attack someone for not selling you an item.
- You shoot law enforcement randomly to start chaos.
- You kill someone because “they are from another gang” with no interaction.

---

## 4.2 Revenge Killing

- If you are **PK'd**, you do not remember the events leading to your death.
- You **cannot return to the scene or seek revenge**.
- Waiting OOC until enemies log off, then attacking them = Rule Break.
- CK removes your character permanently — no revenge or continuation.

---

## 4.3 Robberies, Kidnappings & Hostage Situations

Allowed if:
- There is a believable IC reason.  
- The location is realistic and not heavily crowded (no main roads, PD stations, hospitals, spawn points).  
- You give the victim time to RP (use /me /do properly).  
- You have a plan to escape realistically.

Forbidden if:
- Robbing players at scripted job locations (fishing spots, trucking depots).  
- Robbing someone right after they spawn or leave an interior.  
- Robbing in front of CCTV, police stations, or crowded areas without reason.  
- Forcing players to show inventory or abuse script to steal unrealistic items (bank cards, 500k cash, etc.)  
- Kidnapping EMS, firefighters or police **just to steal equipment**.

---

## 4.4 Hostage Rules

- You must have a reason (revenge, intimidation, information, debt, criminal plot).
- You must treat the hostage realistically — no random executions.
- Negotiations with BCSO should be in-character and serious.
- You may only kill a hostage if:
  - They attempt escape.
  - Law enforcement initiates dangerous breach.
  - You have admin approval for execution-style CK.

---

## 4.5 Police Chases & Surrendering

- If you are being chased by BCSO and escape realistically → fine.
- If you crash and your vehicle is disabled → you **cannot run like nothing happened**.
- Jumping from cliffs, driving into water intentionally to escape = **Powergaming**.
- If multiple firearms are aimed at you → **you must surrender** unless you have a clear tactical advantage.

**Allowed:**  
- Running on foot if vehicle breaks down.  
- Hiding if you lost police visually.  
- Surrendering with hands up: `/me raises hands slowly`.

**Not allowed:**  
- Disconnecting to avoid arrest (**LTA**).  
- Respawning during chase.  
- Pretending crash was “lag” to avoid RP.

---

## 4.6 Combat Logging (LTA)

Combat Logging (LTA) = leaving the game to avoid arrest, death, robbery, or consequences.

You are combat logging if:
- You quit/alt+f4 during a police arrest, robbery or shootout.
- You crash and don’t return within 15 minutes.
- You fake lag or AFK to avoid interaction.

**Punishment:** Admin jail → ban (for repeat).

---

## 4.7 Weapon Use & Shootouts

- Guns are **deadly weapons** — pulling one must always be justified.
- Shooting must be the **last resort**, not the first.
- No “spray & pray”, no running in circles while shooting like GTA Online.
- Long guns (rifles, shotguns) must be properly roleplayed taking out.
- You cannot pull weapons while on a motorcycle at 100km/h.
- If your gun is drawn first at close range, you cannot pull your weapon back unless:
  - They are distracted.
  - You have cover.
  - You use deception.
- For pistols and small weapons, there is no need to roleplay pulling it out except in cases where there is need to show how it was done.
