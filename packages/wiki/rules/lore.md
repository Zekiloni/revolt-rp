# 11 Lore & Continuity

Revolt Roleplay takes place in **San Andreas (Blaine County, Sandy Shores, Grapeseed, Paleto Bay)** within the GTA V universe.

To protect realism, all players must respect server lore and continuity.

---

## 11.1 Setting & Time

- The world is **modern-day USA**, based on the GTA universe.
- American laws, culture and social norms apply.
- **Real-life events (wars, pandemics, politicians)** do not automatically exist IC unless staff announces it.
- Time moves **at the same speed as real life time** (1 day IRL = 1 day IC).

---

## 11.2 Character Lore Compliance

Your character **must fit into GTA V universe**, meaning:
- Realistic birthplace (USA + San Andreas or immigrant with reason).  
- No real-life celebrities, streamers, YouTubers or duplicates.  
- No supernatural beings (vampires, angels, superheroes, aliens).  
- No real-life political figures like “Vladimir Putin”, “Trump”, etc.  

---

## 11.3 Vehicle, Weapon & Technology Lore

Allowed:
- Normal civilian cars, pickup trucks, ATVs, dirt bikes.
- Legal firearms according to San Andreas laws.
- Mobile phones, internet, social media **if roleplayed realistically**.

Not allowed:
- Futuristic weapons, laser guns, military tanks or jets (unless admin event).
- Real-life brands like “BMW, Mercedes” — must use **GTA lore brands**:  
  - BMW = Übermacht  
  - Mercedes = Benefactor  
  - Chevrolet = Declasse  
  - Ford = Vapid  
- Real-life companies like McDonald's, Facebook → use Cluckin’ Bell, Lifeinvader etc.

---

## 11.4 Breaking Lore = NonRP

Examples:
- “My character is from Russia GRU and trained with Spetsnaz and CIA.”  
- Driving a Lamborghini Aventador in 200-population Sandy Shores as a 19-year-old.  
- Claiming Elon Musk is president of San Andreas.  
- Surviving 10 bullets because "my character is ex-military".
