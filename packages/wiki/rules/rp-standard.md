# 3 Roleplay Standards & Character Rules

This section defines how characters should behave, develop, and deal with consequences such as injuries, arrests, CK and PK.

---

## 3.1 Character Development

- Every character must have a realistic **background, personality and goal.**
- Characters must act according to their **age, upbringing, mental state, skills and fears.**
- Your character is **not a superhero**. No military training, special forces past or high-level criminal connections without admin approval.
- Characters should change over time based on their **IC experiences** (trauma, arrests, injuries, success, etc.)
- Switching emotions instantly (from crying to joking after a friend dies) is **NonRP**.

---

## 3.2 CK and PK – Character Death Rules

### **PK (Player Kill)** – *Temporary death*

- Your character “forgets” the events leading to death.
- You may keep your character, assets, memories **before** the situation.
- You cannot return to the scene (untill the scene has been concluded) or seek revenge for what happened.

### **CK (Character Kill)** – *Permanent death*

- Character is permanently dead and cannot be used again.
- All properties, money and faction roles are lost.
- You must create a **new character with a different name and story.**
- CKs may only be:
  - Approved by administration, or
  - Authorized by specific faction rules (e.g. Illegal, Police Internal Affairs...) with staff oversight.

---

## 3.3 When is CK Forced?

CK may be enforced if:
- You jump off buildings or suicide to avoid jail/punishment.
- You commit "suicide by cop" (intentionally dying to escape arrest).
- You exploit a situation to die instead of RP consequences.
- A criminal organization places a valid **CK contract** with admin approval.
- You die during a **major storyline or court case** and staff decide CK is logical.

---

## 3.4 Injuries & Medical Roleplay (No Wounded Timer System)

Medical RP must be realistic and properly roleplayed.

### Rules:
- If your character is shot, stabbed, hit by a car or seriously injured → you **must roleplay pain and injuries realistically.**
- You cannot instantly walk, run, jump or fight after being shot or crashed.
- You may seek help from **Blaine County Fire & Rescue / medical personnel**.
- If no medical staff are online, you must:
  - Roleplay self-aid or treatment by another player.
  - Continue injury roleplay for a reasonable time.
- Refusing to RP injuries or acting invincible is **Powergaming**.

---

## 3.5 Suicide Roleplay

- Self-harm or suicide RP is allowed only with **serious character development and admin approval**.
- It must never be used to:
  - Avoid jail or consequences.
  - Gain attention or troll.
- If your character commits suicide, it is considered **CK** unless otherwise approved.

If you are facing difficulties in real life, please seek help, we are an open community and note that we will always be there for you
as a place where you can forget about your problems and build your character and story as you want. Communication is the key to success.

---

## 3.6 Memory, Trauma & Consequences

Your character must react realistically to trauma:

| Situation | Expected Roleplay |
|-----------|--------------------|
| You survive a shooting | Fear, anxiety, possible PTSD, avoidance of danger zones. |
| You witness a brutal murder | Shock, fear, emotional reaction, possible nightmares. |
| Your close friend/relative dies | Grief, sadness, potential behavioral changes. |
| You spend long time in prison | Possible changes in behavior, mentality, gang affiliations. |

---

## 3.7 Character Kill Appeals

- You can appeal a CK only if:
  - It was done without admin approval.
  - There was no valid IC reason or escalation.
  - There is proof of metagaming/powergaming.

Appeals are handled on forum by staff team.

---

## 3.8 Names & Identity

**Allowed:**
- Realistic first + last names (e.g. *Michael Hayes, Sofia Alvarez, Ivan Markovic*)
- Ethnic names that match the character’s background

**Not allowed:**
- Single names or celebrity names (e.g. *Drake, Eminem, John Wick*)
- Joke names or meme names (*Mike Hawk, Hugh Janus, Ben Dover*)
- “Gangster-style” fake names as legal identity (*LilTrigger, XXSniperBloodXX*)
- Real-life political figures or historical characters

---

## 3.9 Appearance Realism

Your character’s model must fit their story and background.

 Allowed:
- Normal body proportions  
- Imperfections: scars, acne, wrinkles, tattoos, realistic makeup  
- Visible signs of lifestyle (bad teeth for junkies, luxury look for rich characters, etc.)

 Not allowed:
- Anime-style hair colors (neon blue, purple, pink), unless IC-dyed and roleplayed realistically  
- Face tattoos that cover the entire face (unless IC earned & believable)  
- Fantasy or supernatural details: glowing eyes, demon horns, vampire skin  
- Impossibly muscular/fat proportions that break physics  

---

## 3.10 Clothing & Outfits

- Outfits must match character income, gang affiliation, job or lifestyle  
- Police/firefighters must wear uniforms only while on duty  
- Gang members wearing tactical military gear is not allowed unless properly roleplayed  
- No running around in underwear, towels or swimsuits outside beaches or homes (without proper IC reason)
- Wearing masks or helmets in public without reason is considered suspicious ICly  

---

## 3.11 Scars, Disabilities & Medical Accuracy

You may roleplay:
- Scars, burns, amputations, prosthetic limbs  
- Blindness, deafness, wheelchairs, PTSD, trauma  
- Chronic illness or psychological conditions  

But only if:
- You roleplay them consistently  
- You understand the condition (no mocking / no superpowers)  
- You **don’t use disabilities for OOC advantage or trolling**

---

## 3.12 Changing Character Appearance

Minor appearance changes:
- Hairstyles, beard, clothing — can be changed freely IC at barber/shop  

Major appearance changes (must be roleplayed):
- Removing face tattoos  
- Gender transition  
- Plastic surgery, scar removal  
- Significant weight gain/loss or aging effects  

These changes require:
- Proper RP (medical procedure, healing time)  
- IC money cost (clinic, surgery, etc.)  
- **Admin approval if drastic (identity change, witness protection, etc.)**

---

## 3.13 Lore & Cultural Consistency

Characters must follow GTA lore:
- Cities, cars, brands should be GTA versions (e.g. Los Santos, Vapid, Los Santos Customs — not LA, Ford, BMW)  
- No real-life religions/terrorist groups directly — adapted lore versions only (`Christians = Los Santos Catholic Church`, `Muslim = San Andreas Islamic Society`, etc.)  
- No foreign princes, international spies, presidents, navy seals unless approved by staff

---

## 3.14 Violations & Punishments

If a character violates customization rules:
1. Admin may ask to change appearance or name  
2. If ignored → forced model change or name-change (NC)  
3. Repeated violations → warns, kicks or bans  

---

**Characters should look and act like real people. You are creating a person, not a parody.**
