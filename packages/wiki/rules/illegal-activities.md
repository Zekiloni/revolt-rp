# 8 Robbing, Scamming & Anti-Crime Regulations

This section describes rules regarding robbery, scamming, property crimes, restricted zones and firearm license abuse. Crime roleplay is allowed and encouraged on Revolt Roleplay but must remain realistic, fair and respectful to other players.

---

## 8.1 Mugging (Street Robbery)

Allowed if:
- There is a realistic reason and opportunity.
- The area is not crowded or under CCTV/police surveillance.
- You give the victim time to respond with /me and /do.

Not allowed:
- Mugging in front of police stations, banks, hospitals, spawn areas or government buildings.
- Mugging someone the moment they log in, exit a property or are in a loading screen.
- Forcing a player to /showinventory or scrolling through entire inventory unrealistically.
- Mugging unrealistic ammounts of cash, drugs or weapons. (Try to keep it low.)

---

## 8.2 Robbery (Businesses, Stores, Banks)

- Must involve proper planning and valid IC reason.
- Coordination with at least 1–2 players.
- You must roleplay fear of alarms, CCTV, police response.
- No robbing 24/7 stores every 8 minutes “for cash grinding.”
- Hostage-taking is allowed but must be serious and realistic and in accordance to rules.
- Staff may intervene if the situation becomes non-RP or trolling.
- The same business can be robbed max. once in 3 days.

---

## 8.3 Burglary (House/Property Break-ins)

Allowed only if:
- The target is realistically scouted (observed habits, unlocked doors, IC intel).  
- You roleplay lockpicking or forced entry properly.  
- You do not steal unrealistic item amounts (no looting everything, 200 guns, 500k in cash etc).  
- You do not break into faction properties unless an active RP story exists.

Not allowed:
- Breaking in while the owner is offline just to loot.  
- Entering interiors via glitches / animations / map exploits.  
- Stealing every single item to grief the player.  

---

## 8.4 Scams (Fraud RP)

- Scams are allowed but must be entirely IC.
- The scam must not exceed a realistic or reasonable amount (excessive wealth removal, over $25.000 = staff review).
- Scamming using OOC methods is forbidden and both parties will be punished:
  - “Send me money on PayPal I send you car IC”  
  - Using Discord lies and then asking for IC money  
- You cannot scam faction funds, government accounts or scripted job payments.

---

## 8.5 General Rules for Robbing & Scamming

- Must involve clear roleplay.  
- Victim must have a chance to react (/me, /do).  
- You may take cash, phones, small weapons, drugs.  
- You may NOT take:
- Properties, vehicles, bank accounts.
- Entire inventories or script assets (company ownership, bank PINs, etc.)
- You may NOT kill someone **just because they don’t have money**.

---

## 8.6 Property Destruction (Arson, Vandalism)

Allowed if:
- There is a valid IC motive.
- You roleplay preparation (gasoline, tools, accelerants).
- You notify admins.

Not allowed:
- Burning player houses for OOC revenge.  
- Random destruction of public buildings or faction HQs.  
- Arson without planning or consequences.  

---

## 8.7 Anti-Crime Zones

Crime is strictly forbidden in:
- Active sheriff offices, courthouses et cetera.
- Government buildings (City Hall, Licensing Office).
- Hospitals, medical clinics, fire stations.
- Spawn points or other similar areas.

Robbery, kidnapping or killing in these zones = immediate admin punishment.

---

## 8.8 Abusing a Purchase Firearm License (PF License)

- PF License is for (example) **legal self-defense and sport shooting only**.
- You may NOT:
  - Buy guns legally and give/sell them to criminals.  
  - Use PF weapons for robberies or gang wars.  
  - Roleplay “losing” a gun repeatedly to supply others.  
- PF License can be suspended IC by BCSO or revoked OOC by admins.  
- Repeated abuse = PF blacklist + weapon seizure + admin jail.
