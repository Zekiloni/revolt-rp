# 7 Illegal Factions & Criminal Roleplay

Illegal factions on Revolt Roleplay (gangs, cartels, MC clubs, mafia, drug organizations etc.) must follow strict realism and server rules. Criminal RP is of course allowed and encouraged — but it must be intelligent, developed and believable.

This includes:
- **Motorcycle Clubs**
- **Street Gangs**
- **Drug Trafficking Groups**
- **Cartels / Mafia-style Organizations**
- **Other**

### Rules:
- Must be **approved by Faction Management**.
- Unofficial groups can start their roleplay and faction thread, but FM keeps their right to close the faction due to subpar standards.
- Must follow **realistic structure and hierarchy**.
- Extortion, racketeering, drug trade must create RP, not only profit.
- CK agreements can exist within factions **but are enforced only with admin approval** and are reserved for official factions.
- No forced recruitment.
- Unofficial factions can request official status at anytime, but FM keeps the right to decline based on facts and evidence.

---

## 7.1 Creating & Managing an Illegal Faction

To start an illegal organization (unofficial), you must:
- Have at least **3 active members and a clear concept**.
- Open faction thread and present your faction and roleplay accordingly.
- Follow realistic background, culture, hierarchy and activity.

Factions must:
- Create development roleplay, not only violence.  
- Keep screenshots / logs of RP for faction thread or proof.  
- Enforce character development and CK rules properly.  

To start any unofficial (illegal) faction, please keep in mind that you should start from zero. No spawning in as capos or bosses.
Start a small crew and see where it leads you. Any unrealistic factions will be closed by FM.

---

## 7.2 Illegal Activities (Allowed & Not Allowed)

**Allowed criminal roleplay includes:**
- Drug trafficking, small-time dealing, labs.
- Gun running, illegal weapon trades.
- Extortion, racketeering, protection schemes.
- Territory disputes, gang feuds (must be escalated RP).
- Robbery, kidnapping (with realistic planning).

**Not allowed:**
- Random terror attacks, bombings, mass shootings without admin permission.  
- Forcing people to join gangs (kidnapping → forced recruitment).  
- Extorting every business immediately after opening.  
- Ethnic/racial extremist roleplay without admin approval.

---

## 7.3 Turf Wars & Conflict Rules

- Gangs may claim **“turf”**, but only through RP, graffiti, influence & presence.
- Spraying graffiti or tagging must be roleplayed — not spammed.
- Major wars between factions must be reported to Faction Management.
- Drive-by shootings are allowed **only** when there is strong escalation.

---

## 7.4 Informants & Snitches

**Snitch / Criminal Informant RP is allowed**, BUT:
- Must be **100% IC** — no metagaming.
- Criminal factions can **CK informants** — ONLY with admin approval and valid proof.
- You cannot force someone OOC to stop informing — only IC consequences allowed.

---

## 7.5 CK Rules in Criminal RP

A character may be CK’d if (examples):
- They betray the faction (informant, stealing from organization).  
- Leader orders a CK with admin approval.  
- They accept a **CK agreement** before joining the faction. (Official factions)
- Death is meaningful to the story (not rage-killing).

**How CK happens in illegal RP:**
1. Faction leadership submits valid reasoning to staff.  
2. Staff approve or deny.  
3. Character is killed IC → must name-change and delete storyline.

---

## 7.6 Criminal Kidnapping / Hostage Rules

Allowed when:
- Used for interrogation, debt collection, revenge or intimidation etc. 
- Victim is treated realistically (fear, negotiation).  

Not allowed:
- Kidnapping random civilians for no reason.  
- Hostage-taking just to shoot cops.  
- Torture RP without **victim’s OOC consent**.

---

## 7.7 Drug & Weapon Roleplay

- Drugs must have **supply chains, production or import RP**.  
- No “spawned drugs” or magical suppliers.  
- Weapon deals must include proper roleplay (example):
  - `/me shows the crate to buyer.`
  - `/do There would be 4 pistols and 2 shotguns inside.`
- Weapons must be obtained IC — not via alt-characters or OOC trading.

---

## 7.8 Extortion & Protection RP

Allowed if:
- Business owners are approached IC, with reason and planning.  
- Money demanded is reasonable (not $100,000 from local bar or grocery store).  
- Violence is last option — not first.

Not allowed:
- Forcing every business in the city to pay.  
- Burning properties without escalation.  
- Demanding OOC money, items or Discord info.
