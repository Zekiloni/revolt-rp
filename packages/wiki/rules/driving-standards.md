# 6 Vehicles & Driving Rules

Driving on Revolt Roleplay must follow realistic standards. This is a heavy text-based roleplay server – GTA Online / arcade-style behavior is not allowed.

---

## 6.1 General Driving Expectations

Players must:
- Drive according to road laws, character ability and vehicle type.
- Roleplay crashes and mechanical failures realistically.
- Treat roads, dirt paths, off-road terrain and weather realistically.

Players must NOT:
- Drive 120+ km/h through Sandy Shores or residential areas without reason.
- Perform stunts, jumps, drifting in public streets unless ICly appropriate.
- Ram vehicles without roleplay.
- Drive in first-person shooter style (zig-zagging while shooting, jumping with cars).
- Ignore police lights/sirens unless there is a clear IC reason.

---

## 6.2 Vehicle Crashes & Damage

If your vehicle crashes into another car, object, animal or pedestrian, you must stop and roleplay consequences or continue if you have a valid IC reason.

**Example of correct RP:**
- /me loses control and crashes head-first into a street pole.
- /do The front of the vehicle is smashed, smoke comes from the hood.
- /me holds his shoulder in pain, trying to breathe.

Failing to roleplay damage or injuries after a crash is considered **Powergaming**.

If the crash is minor:  
- Acknowledge it with light RP and continue.  
If the crash is major:  
- You must RP injuries, damaged vehicle and possibly call BCSO or BCFR.

---

## 6.3 Police Pursuits (Vehicle)

Pursuits must be realistic, not cinematic.

Allowed:
- Evading because of valid IC reasons (warrants, drugs, illegal weapons).
- Hiding or escaping if realistically possible.
- Using backroads or off-road if your vehicle allows it (SUVs, dirt bikes).
- Surrendering.

Not allowed:
- Jumping off cliffs or mountains with a sports car.
- Driving into lakes or oceans to escape.
- Crashing at 150 km/h and running away uninjured.
- Logging out.

If caught and multiple guns are aimed at you:
- You must show **FearRP** and surrender (unless you logically have an advantage and IC reason to flee).

---

## 6.4 Vehicle Theft & Hotwiring

Stealing vehicles is allowed ONLY if:
- There is a believable IC reason.  
- Location is suitable (not right in front of a police station).  
- You roleplay lockpicking or hotwiring.  
- You do not immediately chop or sell without RP.

**Example:**
- /me kneels near the driver's door, checking for anyone nearby.
- /me uses a screwdriver to tamper with the door lock.
- /do Does the alarm go off? (If there is an owner nearby...).


Forbidden:
- Stealing vehicles randomly "for fun".  
- Stealing emergency vehicles unless part of serious RP or without admin approval.  
- Using vehicle commands or menu commands to bypass RP.

---

## 6.5 Parking & Abandoning Vehicles

- Do not leave vehicles in middle of the road and log off. 
- AFK parking in front of banks, stores, events or hospitals is not allowed. 
- If you are going to be AFK, find an optimal place for that, do not bother other players.

---

## 6.6 Boats, Aircraft & Special Vehicles

- Aircraft use (helicopters, planes) is allowed only for approved government factions or special events.
- Boats must be used realistically (fishing, coast patrol, smuggling with RP).
- Military vehicles, tanks, jets or armored cars are **strictly prohibited** unless admin-approved event.
