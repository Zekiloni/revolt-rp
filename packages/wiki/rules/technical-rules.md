# 12 Technical Rules & Exploit Prevention

These rules cover technical behavior in-game, including disconnects, mods, abusing game scripts or interacting with staff.

---

## 12.1 Bug Abuse & Exploits

It is **strictly forbidden** to use any kind of exploit, glitch or bug to gain an advantage.

This includes:
- Duping money, items or weapons.
- Using map glitches to enter locked buildings.
- Shooting through walls or using camera exploits to see through objects.
- Exploiting animation bugs (cancel reloads) to gain advantage.
- Abusing script commands to escape RP.
- Using third-party programs, macros or cheat software.

**If you discover a bug → report it to staff.**

---

## 12.2 AFK & Idle Behavior

Allowed:
- AFK in private interiors/property.
- AFK at other locations if no active RP is blocked.

Not allowed:
- AFK in public RP areas (shops, spawn zones, hospital).  
- AFK during active roleplay scenes.  

---

## 12.3 Mods, Crosshair & Third-Party Software

**Allowed Mods:**
- Reshade / ENB (graphics only)  
- Sound mods (weapons, vehicles)  
-Visual texture mods that don’t alter balance  

**Forbidden:**
- Aimbots, triggerbots, recoil scripts  
- Crosshair overlays or monitors with marker tape  
- ESP / wallhacks / radar hacks  
- Using mod menus, trainers or Cheat Engine

If unsure about a mod → ask administration before installing.

---

## 12.4 Data Transfer, Alt Characters & Asset Trading

You may **not**:
- Transfer money, weapons, properties or vehicles between your own characters without admin approval.
- Use one character to gain assets illegally and send them to another (alt farming).
- Share account, character or login info with other players.

**Real-World Trading (RWT)** is strictly forbidden:
- No selling IC money, vehicles or properties for real-life currency or services (PayPal, crypto, etc.).
- No “donate to me IRL, I’ll give you 1M in-game”.

---

## 12.5 Admin Interaction & Rule Enforcement

- Admin decisions must be followed immediately — do not argue in RP.
- You may politely appeal AFTER the scene via forum.
- Lying to staff, refusing to RP or ignoring instructions = punishment.
- Recording or streaming admin situations is allowed, but **must not be edited to lie or harass**.

---

## 12.6 Reporting Players

To report someone:
- Use `/report` for urgent issues (active rulebreak, hacking).
- Use forum for non-urgent complaints.
- Always provide evidence (screenshots/logs) and remain respectful.

False reporting, witch hunting or lying in reports can result in penalties.
