# Staff Team Structure

The Revolt Roleplay Staff Team is made of dedicated individuals who ensure the server runs smoothly, fairly and remains loyal to heavy roleplay standards.

---

## 1. Core Administration

| Position | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Founders** | Creators of Revolt Roleplay. Highest authority — final decisions, vision, server direction. |
| **Management** | Senior staff responsible for day-to-day leadership, staff coordination, major decisions and approvals. |
| **Admin Team** | Handles in-game administration, rule enforcement, reports, player support and punishments. |
| **Testers** | Test new features, systems and updates before they go live. Report bugs, provide feedback to development team. Review UCP Applications and provide assistance to players in game and on discord. |

---

## 2. Specialized Teams

| Team | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Development Team** | Programmers, scripters and UI/UX designers. They build, maintain and optimize scripts, systems and new features. |
| **Modding Team** | Responsible for all maps and mods within the community and server. |
| **Faction Management** | Supervises all factions and group roleplay. Divided into: |
| • Illegal Faction Management (IFM) | Oversees gangs, cartels, mafia, MCs. Approves concepts, CKs, wars, illegal roleplay quality. |
| • Legal Faction Management (LFM) | Handles police, fire, medical, government and legal businesses. |
| **Property Team** | Manages houses, businesses, mapping, interiors and property requests (buying, mapping, furnishing). |
| **Roleplay Continuity & Lore Team** | Ensures lore consistency, timeline, server history and realism of stories (character backgrounds, events, factions). |
